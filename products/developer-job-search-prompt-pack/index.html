Job search pack #5

Project to resume bullets

When to use: You built something and need to describe it in one or two lines.

Turn this project into 3 resume bullet options. Use the pattern "did X, using Y, resulting in Z".
Project details: [WHAT IT IS, STACK, WHAT I PERSONALLY BUILT, LINKS, ANY REAL RESULTS: users, speed, tests, stars]

Rules: use only what I wrote. Where there is no result, end with what the project shows (for example "deployed publicly with CI") instead of inventing a number. Give a one-line note on which bullet is most honest and specific.
Job search pack #7

Resume critique as a skeptical recruiter

When to use: You want blunt feedback before you apply.

Act as a busy technical recruiter with 30 seconds per resume for a [ROLE] opening. Here is my resume:
[PASTE]

Tell me: what you took away in the first 10 seconds, what is unclear, which bullets are vague or unbelievable, what is missing for this role, and the first three things you would change. Be direct and kind. Do not rewrite yet.

Who it's for

Early-career and career-changing developers, bootcamp and self-taught graduates, and anyone in a developer job search who wants clear, honest application materials and focused interview practice.

Format and honest notes

PDF. Written with AI assistance and edited. Prompts are templates with [BRACKETS] to fill in, and examples are illustrative. It is a writing and practice aid, not career, legal or financial advice, and it cannot promise interviews or offers. AI tools can be wrong, including about code, so verify answers by running code and checking facts. Remove confidential and personal data before pasting, and do not use AI during live interviews or assessments unless the employer allows it.

Questions

Will the AI invent skills or experience on my resume?

It can, which is why every prompt tells it to use only your evidence, to ask when something is missing and to flag misleading claims. Prompt 06 tailors a resume by reordering and tightening only, and you should compare the result with your master document line by line.

Can I use this during a live interview?

No, and the pack says so. It is for preparing and practising: stories, hints on practice problems, project deep dives and debriefs. Many employers prohibit AI during interviews and assessments, so follow their rules.

Is it only for junior developers?

It is written mainly for early-career and career-changing developers, but the resume, outreach, story and offer prompts work at any level.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Any modern chat assistant that lets you paste a long message. The prompts are plain text.
